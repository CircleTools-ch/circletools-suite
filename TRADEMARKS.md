# Trademark policy

The **code** of CircleTools is open source under the [Apache-2.0 license](LICENSE). The license covers
copyright and patents — **not** our names and logos (its section 6 says so). This page explains what you
may do with them. It is deliberately short; when in doubt, ask.

## What the marks are

- the name **CircleTools** and the domain **circletools.ch**;
- the CircleTools brand mark (the ring with the central glyph) and every app icon and logo in this
  repository — see [brand assets](docs/process/brand-assets.md) for the full list;
- the names of the suite's apps **when they are combined with CircleTools or our logos**, such as
  "Secret Santa · CircleTools". A generic term like "Secret Santa" on its own is not ours, but an app that
  looks like ours under that name is (see below).

## Fine without asking

- **Run it yourself.** Use the software, modified or not, for yourself, your family, your friends, your
  club or your company — including on your own server with the official container images. A self-hosted
  instance may keep our name and logos **inside the running app**.
- **Talk about it.** Say that your project is "based on CircleTools", "a fork of CircleTools" or
  "compatible with CircleTools", in plain text and without our logos.
- **Write about it.** Articles, reviews, tutorials, talks and screenshots that show the real apps.

## Needs a new name and new logos

- **Publishing an app** in any app store (Google Play, App Store, F-Droid, …) built from this code.
- **Offering a public service** — anyone can sign up, or you advertise it beyond your own circle — built from
  this code.
- **Anything that suggests it is official**, endorsed by or connected with CircleTools when it is not.

For these, rename the app and the service, replace every logo and icon, use your own colours, and change
the app and bundle identifiers. [Brand assets](docs/process/brand-assets.md) lists every place that needs
to change. Keep the Apache-2.0 `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` — that is a license condition, not a branding one —
and you are welcome to credit the project in your "About" page.

## Asking for permission

Write to **info@circletools.ch**. We are happy to say yes to things that help users and do not confuse
them.
