# Draw

## Definition

The act (and its result) of randomly assigning each [Member](member.md) a recipient to give a
gift to, honoring all [Exclusions](exclusion.md). A successful draw produces a complete set of
[Assignments](assignment.md).

## Bounded context(s)

- **Secret-Santa:** performed once a group is ready; may be re-run until shared/locked.

## Synonyms / do-not-use

- **Synonyms:** drawing of lots, matching.
- **Do NOT use:** "raffle", "lottery" (imply prizes/chance of winning).
