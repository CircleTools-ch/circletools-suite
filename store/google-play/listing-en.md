## Full description (≤4000)

```
Secret Santa makes organizing a gift exchange effortless — for your family, friends, team, or any group.

Create a group, add everyone, and draw names fairly in seconds. Set exclusions so couples or people who always pair up don't draw each other. Everyone gets a private link that reveals only who they've drawn — and their wishlist — so the secret stays a secret.

Best of all, it works completely offline and needs no account. Nothing to sign up for, and the people you invite don't need an account either — just share a link.

HOW IT WORKS
• Create a group and add members
• Set who can't draw whom (exclusions)
• Draw names with a single tap
• Share each person's private reveal link via WhatsApp, Messages, or email
• Recipients open their link to see who they've got, plus that person's wishlist

FEATURES
• Works offline — no account required
• Free, with no ads
• Wishlists, so everyone knows what to get
• Optional gift budget to keep spending fair
• Exclusions for couples, families, or repeat pairings
• Optional gentle reminders so no one forgets to draw or shop
• Optional account to sync across your devices and reuse your groups year after year

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data stays on your device. There's no cloud and no account unless you choose to sign in for syncing. Reveal links are private, and recipients never need an account.

AVAILABLE IN ENGLISH, GERMAN, AND FRENCH
Switch languages any time from the sidebar.

Whether it's Christmas, an office party, or a family get-together, Secret Santa takes the hassle out of gift exchanges — so you can focus on the fun.
```
